01 · About

Changing how big energy runs on cloud and AI.

My career has followed one question. How does a century-old industry, built on physics, steel and decades of data, rebuild itself on cloud and AI without losing what makes it work?

I have answered it from every seat at the table. I spent a decade inside Chevron as an operator. I then carried that experience to Microsoft, AWS and now Google Cloud, where I set energy technology strategy across the three largest cloud platforms in the world.

Along the way I have led, sponsored and launched programs that reset what the industry thought was possible: Chevron, SLB and Microsoft's Triple Crown partnership, the Shell, SLB and AWS subsurface collaboration, Eni's geoscience platform, and the Energy HPC Orchestrator built with Shell and Occidental.

The pattern is the same each time. Align the operator, the service company and the cloud provider around one outcome. Put domain experts and engineers in the same room. Ship a platform the whole industry can build on.

Amazon Web Services

Director and Chief Technologist, Energy and Utilities · Head of Upstream

I led cloud migration and technology innovation programs for the world's leading energy and utility companies, and served as the senior technology voice of AWS to their executives.

  • Shell, SLB and AWS. Sponsored the multi-year, three-way agreement to deliver end-to-end digital subsurface workflows for Shell, running SLB software on AWS.
  • Energy HPC Orchestrator. Launched an open platform for seismic imaging and reservoir simulation, built with Shell and Occidental and accelerated with NVIDIA. It opened workloads that once took tens of millions of dollars in hardware to operators of any size.
  • Eni geoscience platform. Partnered with Eni on its collaborative geoscience platform, presented together at AWS re:Invent 2023.
  • Generative AI for energy. Co-authored AWS's thesis on generative AI in energy, later cited by the Brookings Institution, and took it to the keynote stage at DISTRIBUTECH, CERAWeek and SPE ATCE.
Earlier

Microsoft

CTO, Energy and Resources · Director, Strategic Customer Engagements

I rose from program manager on Azure's big-compute team to chief technology officer for Microsoft's worldwide energy business, partnering with energy companies around the world on digital transformation and the energy transition.

  • Triple Crown. Led the landmark partnership between Chevron, SLB and Microsoft that put SLB's petrotechnical software on Azure, shaped by Chevron's expertise, and deployed it globally. It became the foundation for a commercial data platform offered to the whole industry.
  • Cloud HPC for oil and gas. Led delivery of Microsoft's cloud high-performance computing platform for the industry, moving seismic processing and reservoir simulation off fixed on-premises clusters.
  • Strategic customers. Directed Microsoft's engagements with its most strategic energy customers, from first architecture to enterprise-wide rollout.
10 yrs

Chevron

Enterprise Architect · Upstream Transformation

A decade inside one of the world's largest operators, across upstream and midstream business units. This is where I learned what technology has to survive in the field.

  • Upstream technology strategy. Shaped Chevron's key technology strategies for drilling and completions, production and operations, and reservoir management.
  • The unconventionals factory. Served as enterprise architect for the information architecture behind Chevron's factory model, mapping the critical data across the full life of a well.
  • The operator's view. Built the perspective I later brought to Triple Crown and every partnership since: start from the asset, not the platform.
